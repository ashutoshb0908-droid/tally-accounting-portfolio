# Corporate Financial Accounting & Data Audit — Tally Prime

## ₹ Project Overview
This portfolio project demonstrates practical execution of end-to-end small business accounting and compliance workflows using **Tally Prime** and **Microsoft Excel**. It highlights the lifecycle of transaction records for **Neelkanth Enterprises** across the 2026-2027 fiscal year, moving from original journal entry vouchers to audited year-end financial summaries.

---

## ₹ Core Project Objectives
* **Double-Entry Verification:** Record and cross-verify cross-ledger journal accounts including asset acquisitions, vendor payables, and drawings.
* **Financial Auditing:** Analyze trading accounts to evaluate operating margins (Gross Profit) and evaluate net bottom-line variances (Net Loss).
* **Data Migration:** Recreate systematic Tally database reports inside an executive-ready, cleanly formatted Excel presentation ledger.

---

## ₹ Tools Used
* **Tally Prime:** For database management, inventory control posting, voucher entries (Payment, Receipt, Contra), and automated ledger balance generation.
* **Microsoft Excel:** For formatting financial data models, applying accounting cell-border styles, and cleaning structural strings.

---

## ₹ Technical Skills & Ledger Framework Demonstrated

### 1. Voucher Mapping Rules
* **Contra Vouchers:** Executed liquid fund positioning across internal cash reservoirs and commercial bank registers (e.g., Bank of Baroda).
* **Payment Vouchers:** Managed recurring operating overhead distributions (Salaries, Electricity, Commission, Van Maintenance) alongside vendor account clearing entries (Ashoka Furnitures).

### 2. Financial Accounting Statements Audited
* **Trading Account:** Monitored baseline inventory transactions with total **Sales Accounts of ₹600,800.00** against **Purchases of ₹500,000.00**, resolving to a **Gross Profit of ₹100,800.00**.
* **Profit & Loss A/c:** Evaluated downstream administrative impacts highlighting **₹246,100.00 in Indirect Expenses** culminating in a **Net Loss of ₹134,300.00**.
* **Balance Sheet Validation:** Reconciled Capital accounts (₹1,350,000.00) against multi-tier asset categories summing to a total balanced threshold of **₹1,526,910.00**.
